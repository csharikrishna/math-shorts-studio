## 2. Confirmed Defects, Disconnected Subsystems & Audit Findings

### A. Disconnected & Broken Theme / CLI / Design System
1. **Fragmented Theme Registry & Forced Dark Coercion:**
   - `studio/config.py` (`THEMES`) only defines `midnight_studio` and `obsidian_chalk` (both dark). `studio/design/spec.py` (`NAMED_LOOKS`) separately defines 4 looks (`midnight_studio`, `obsidian_chalk`, `cartesian_stone`, `editorial_paper`), hardcoding light palettes disconnected from `studio/config.py`.
   - `studio/pipeline.py` `--theme` rejects light themes, and `studio/renderers/manimgx_backend.py#L50`, `studio/longform.py#L513`, and `studio/longform.py#L649` explicitly coerce any non-dark look back to `"midnight_studio"`. Passing `light` or `editorial_paper` into `run_pipeline()` raises `ValueError: Unknown theme`.
2. **Font Inconsistency Without Asset Pinning:**
   - `studio/config.py` defaults `CAPTION_FONT`/`HEADER_FONT` to `"Segoe UI"` (Windows-only), whereas `studio/design/spec.py` specifies `Inter`/`Consolas`. No unified SIL-OFL font pack (`Inter`, `Source Sans 3`, `STIX Two Math`, `Noto Sans`) is pinned for both ManimGX (`font_paths`) and HyperFrames (`@font-face`).

### B. Visual & Layout Defects Verified Against Reference Video (`LF_eigenvalues_...`)
Inspecting `build/audit_ref/lf_audit_1.png`, `lf_audit_2.png`, `CH1.json`, and `CH5.json` explains every confirmed issue:
1. **Tiny Diagrams (~18% Width) & Wasted Frame Space:**
   - `SafeLayout.from_config()` ([studio/layout/components.py#L29-L71](file:///c:/Users/cshar/Downloads/math_shorts_studio/studio/layout/components.py#L29-L71)) permanently reserves `24%` top header + `14%` bottom caption + `6%` side + `6%` gaps even when scenes have no header banner, shrinking `stage_h` to `3.85` units (`48%` of `frame_h=8.0`).
   - Then `scenes/v3_scene.py#L379` caps `equal_aspect=True` axes at `y_len = stage_h * 0.66 = 2.54` units -> `x_len = 2.54` out of `14.222` = **`17.86%` of 1920px frame width**, creating an ~80% empty black void.
2. **Empty Visual Stages During Narration:**
   - `CH5.json:scene_1` (`12s` narration) declares ONLY `main_axes` (`"objects": [main_axes], "constructions": [], "claims": []`) — zero vectors, orbits, or visual demonstrations appear while captions stay on screen.
3. **Label Clipping & Equation Collisions:**
   - `lf_audit_1.png` top-center shows `Re(lambda) > 0: Unstable Sou...` clipped at right margin (`scenes/v3_scene.py#L464-L499` computes `side_x` without clamping inside `safe_w` right/left edges).
   - `lf_audit_2.png` top-right shows `Stability Map: Trace(A), Det(A)` card colliding directly over `\lambda = \alpha \pm i\beta`, and faint fading `What governs path geometry?` / `det(A - \lambda I) = 0` dark-on-dark remnants.
4. **Disconnected, Harsh Shorts-Style Caption Engine:**
   - `CaptionCard` ([studio/layout/components.py#L269-L631](file:///c:/Users/cshar/Downloads/math_shorts_studio/studio/layout/components.py#L269-L631)) draws a full-width permanent dark dock/pill with aggressive yellow background fills on every active word, bypassing prose line comfort, while `HyperFramesBackend` ignores `build_semantic_captions()` and slices into rigid 6-word JS arrays.
5. **Target Duration Overrun (+27.5%):**
   - `longform.py` prompts `2.5 words/s` with a strict minimum (`2.0 words/s`), extends via `extra_idx` loop below `0.96 * target`, and has **no upper budget/pacing trim**, while Kokoro `-4%` speaks ~`2.05–2.2` words/sec + pauses -> `688.62s` actual vs `540.0s` target (`+27.5%`).

### C. Creative, Dialogue, Voice & Registry Gaps
1. **Long-Form Bypasses `CreativeDirector` / `ScriptIR`:**
   - `create_longform_plan()` never produces `STORYLINES.md` or `SCRIPT.md`; chapter scenes invent narration ad-hoc during JSON schema generation instead of compiling from a locked `ScriptIR`.
   - `create_v3_plan()` allows the `VideoPlanV3` LLM call to alter `ScriptIR` narration silently without logging a `ScriptRevision`.
2. **Voice Role Overwrite Bug (`pipeline.py#L102-L106`, `longform.py#L374-L377`, `hyperframes_backend.py`):**
   - Iterating `for prof in DIALOGUE_PROFILES.values(): for sp in prof.speakers: sp_voices[sp.role] = ...` causes `documentary`'s `"narrator"` (`bm_george`) to silently overwrite `single_educator`'s `"narrator"` (`am_michael`), and `technical_instructor`'s `"educator"` (`am_adam`) to overwrite `socratic`/`conversational_explainer`! Furthermore, LLM outputs with capitalized `"speaker": "Narrator"` miss lowercase dictionary lookups.
3. **Non-Executable Component Registry & Silent Animation Fallbacks:**
   - `studio/registry/base.py` only stores descriptive strings; `_compile_event()` ([scenes/v3_scene.py#L269-L327](file:///c:/Users/cshar/Downloads/math_shorts_studio/scenes/v3_scene.py#L269-L327)) silently turns `group` into `m.Indicate()`, `zoom` into `mob.animate.scale(1.18)`, `trace` into plain `m.Create()`, and un-tracked `iterate_states`/`interpolate_values` into `m.Indicate()`, pretending unsupported animations worked.
4. **Monolithic 1,247-line `HyperFramesBackend`:**
   - Single module holds HTTP server, CDP client, theme CSS, SVG generator, JS timing runtime, layout auditor, and FFmpeg muxer. Needs modularization + explicit capability matrix.
5. **Mathematical Imprecision in Stability Analysis:**
   - Plans and prompts lack explicit validation rules distinguishing `all Re(lambda) < 0` (asymptotic sink), `any Re(lambda) > 0` (unstable, which includes **saddles** when signs differ rather than always "unstable source"), pure imaginary conjugates (real 2D **center**), and zero-real-part non-hyperbolic / nonlinear caveats.

### D. Quality Assurance & Cache Integrity Failures
1. **False `100.0/100` Scoring (`studio/qa/engine.py`, `studio/ai/validate_v3.py`):**
   - `verify_v3_plan_detailed()` counts scenes with **no claims** as `ai_reviewed` (`validate_v3.py#L527`), uses `require_claims=False` in `audit_educational_quality()`, and sets `human_review_required=1` rather than count, while `audit_educational_quality()` ignores `ai_reviewed`, omits decoded video frame inspection, and defaults unevaluated visual/storyboard checks to `layout_ok = True` -> `score = 100.0/100` on visibly defective videos!
2. **Cache Integrity Bypass (`studio/longform.py#L422`):**
   - `cached = stage_path if stage_hit else jobs.get_cached(ckey)` **falls back to unverified `jobs.get_cached()` even when `ArtifactCache.lookup()` returned `hit=False` (`checksum_mismatch` or `dependency_or_payload_changed`)**, defeating SHA-256 verification!
   - `chapter_cache_key()` mixes theme/UI/look together with TTS/math model keys instead of separating theme-independent stages (`script`, `math_model`, `tts`) from theme/caption-dependent render stages (`scene_render`, `chapter_assembly`).
3. **Legacy V1/V2 & Documentation Drift:**
   - `README.md` still documents only V1 `ShortPlan` CLI options; `director.py` retains dead V1 constants (`DEFAULT_MODELS`, etc.) alongside `studio/providers/`; `scripts/build_longform_demo.py` duplicates chapter builders superseded by `studio/longform.py`. V1/V2 compatibility should remain callable for legacy `--plan` inputs while clearly isolated behind V3.

## 3. Target Integration Decisions (Phases B–H)

1. **Authoritative Theme & Typography System (`studio/design/` + `studio/config.py`):** Unify all looks under one contrast-checked palette contract featuring `light` (`editorial_light`: `#F7F6F2` ground, `#172033` ink, `#64748B` muted, `#245BD4` primary accent, `#087F75` secondary accent), `dark` (`cinematic_dark`: `#0D1421` ground, `#F1F5F9` ink, `#94A3B8` muted, `#60A5FA` primary, `#5EEAD4` secondary), plus legacy aliases (`editorial_paper`, `cartesian_stone`, `midnight_studio`, `obsidian_chalk`) supported uniformly across CLI, ManimGX, and HyperFrames. Pin SIL-OFL font assets locally in `assets/fonts/`.
2. **Content-Aware Adaptive Layout (`studio/layout/solver.py`):** Collapse empty header/caption reserves dynamically, scale primary visualizations to fill `50–80%` of available stage width/height where appropriate while honoring `equal_aspect`, clamp all annotations strictly within safe zone bounds, and prevent equation card collisions.
3. **Unified `CaptionIR` & Multi-Backend `CaptionStyle` (`studio/captions/`):** Replace the 3 fragmented implementations with one canonical `CaptionIR` supporting `editorial` (default long-form), `kinetic` (short-form), `minimal`, `dialogue`, `accessible`, and `off`, sharing identical phrase breaks across ManimGX, HyperFrames, SRT, WebVTT, ASS, and JSON.
4. **Authoritative `ScriptIR` & Natural Dialogue Flow:** Route both short-form and long-form through `ConceptAnalysis -> CreativeBrief -> ScriptIR -> EditorialReview -> Locked Beat IDs -> VideoPlanV3`, resolving speaker voices cleanly per active `DialogueProfile` and using Podcastfy/HyperFrames lessons without robotic filler.
5. **Executable Component Registry & Modular HyperFrames Adapter:** Upgrade `ComponentSpec` with typed Pydantic contracts, computation hooks, validation hooks, ManimGX/HyperFrames render bindings, explicit capability errors (never silent `Indicate` fallbacks), scene-level backend selection, and modular `studio/renderers/hyperframes/` submodules.
6. **Rigorous Stability Math & Real Decoded Frame QA:** Enforce exact 2D dynamical stability classifications (negative real parts = asymptotic sink, positive real part = unstable [saddle vs unstable node/spiral], pure imaginary = center, zero real part = non-hyperbolic caveats), add decoded-frame OpenCV/PIL inspection in `studio/qa/`, require explicit coverage & non-missing required checks before approval, and fix cache fallback bypass + theme-independent reuse.

## 4. V3.8 Architecture Resolution Summary (`main`)

Audited and resolved directly in V3.8 (`docs/BASELINE_V38_AUDIT.md`):

- **Authoritative Script-First Workflow (`studio/longform.py`, `studio/creative/script_ir.py`, `studio/creative/director.py`):** `create_longform_plan()` now completes `ConceptAnalysis -> CreativeBrief -> Global ScriptIR -> Editorial Review -> Immutable ScriptRevision (SHA-256) -> Scene Compilation` with `strict_ids=True` (no positional beat fallback), clean separation of conceptual `anchor_marks`/`anchor_token_offsets` from `spoken_text`, and automatic rejection of unpronounced code identifiers (`detect_unpronounced_identifiers()`) and repeated transition stems (`detect_narrative_anti_patterns()`).
- **Enforceable Executable Component Registry (`studio/registry/`):** Every `ComponentSpec` now enforces typed Pydantic input/output models, `test_fn`, and `validate_fn` / `compute_fn` + `render_implementations` bindings via `verify_registry_contracts()`, excluding uncertified specs from `capabilities_prompt_summary()`.
- **Renderer-Independent `SceneRuntimeState` (`studio/renderers/scene_state.py`, `studio/renderers/hybrid.py`, `studio/renderers/hyperframes/`):** `evaluate_plan_states()` tracks coordinates, rotations, scales, parameters, and `removed_ids` across `ManimGX <-> HyperFrames`, while `HyperFrames` compiles `move`/`rotate`/`scale`/`zoom` keyframes directly into SVG transforms and `scenes/v3_scene.py` preserves retired persisted curves in `_hidden_persisted`.
- **Canonical Single-Compiled `CaptionIR` (`studio/captions/`, `studio/timeline.py`, `studio/audio/tts.py`, `studio/qa/engine.py`):** `MasterTimeline.export_subtitles()` compiles `caption_ir.json` once from `AudioTimeline` with word-level `TimingProvenance` (`forced_aligned`, `tts_word_boundary`, `interpolated`, `estimated`) and shares identical cues across SRT, WebVTT, ASS, ManimGX (`CaptionCard`), HyperFrames, and `studio/preview.py`.
- **Strict Publication Gates & Scene-Aware Frame QA (`studio/qa/engine.py`, `studio/qa/frames.py`, `studio/storyboard/model.py`):** Separated `ProductionState` (`draft`, `rendered`, `review_required`, `rejected`, `publication_approved`), blocked `approved=True` when `unsupported > 0` or `human_review_required > 0` or unrendered (`approved=True` Markov baseline dropped honestly to `review_required` / `13.0/100`), enforced real Markov stochastic invariants (`is_row_stochastic_matrix`, `verify_stationary_distribution`, `verify_graph_stochastic_weights`), and replaced 9-frame averaging with `inspect_video_scene_aware()` (opening, every scene start/mid/end, bookmarks, backend switches, final + JSON telemetry).
- **12-Layer Dependency Caching & Local Scene Preview (`studio/storage/cache.py`, `studio/preview.py`):** Added `compute_dependency_fingerprints()` + `validate_sidecar_bundle()` plus an offline HTML/loopback (`127.0.0.1`) scene workbench (`build_scene_preview_bundle`, `slice_plan_for_scene`, `render_single_scene_preview`).
